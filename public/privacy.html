Privacy policy

Last updated: .

This policy explains how Rico's Peri Peri ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use our website and order food from us. We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

The data controller is the limited company trading as Rico's Peri Peri, registered in England & Wales. Trading address: 49 Blossom Street, York YO24 1AZ. You can contact us at the phone or email shown on our website. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.

What data we collect

  • Order data: name, email, phone number, delivery address (where applicable), the items in your order and the time of ordering.
  • Payment data: if you pay by card, payment is processed by Stripe — we never see or store your card number. We retain a transaction reference and the last four digits Stripe shares with us.
  • Marketing preferences: we record your marketing choices (email and/or SMS) along with the timestamp. Email marketing is pre-selected at checkout for customers placing an order (you can opt out there or anytime after); SMS marketing is only ever sent if you actively tick the SMS box.
  • Technical data: minimal access logs (IP address, page requested, time) for security and abuse prevention. We use a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not set cookies.

Why we use it

  • To take, prepare and deliver your order — contract.
  • To send order confirmation and "ready time" emails — contract.
  • To meet our legal obligations (e.g. tax records) — legal obligation.
  • To send marketing about offers and new menu items. For email, we rely on the "soft opt-in" — you're an existing customer ordering similar products, the box is pre-selected, and you can opt out at checkout or via the unsubscribe link in any email (legitimate interests / soft opt-in). For SMS, we only send if you actively opt in (consent); reply STOP to withdraw at any time.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse — legitimate interests.

Who we share it with

Our processors, only as needed to run the service:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and DDoS protection.
  • Stripe — card payment processing.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Twilio — SMS delivery (only if you have opted in to SMS marketing).

We never sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: 6 years (HMRC requirement).
  • Marketing preferences: until you unsubscribe, plus 2 years for evidence of consent.
  • Analytics data: aggregated only.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Email us using the address on our website to exercise any of these. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. The site sets one essential cookie when staff log in to the orders dashboard; it is not used for marketing.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you we'll update the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted on the home page.
