Allergy information

Last updated: 26 May 2026.

If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please tell us before you order. Speak to us {{contactLine}} and we will talk you through what is and isn't safe for you. Please do not rely on the menu descriptions alone.

At {{shopName}} we take allergies and intolerances seriously, and we want you to be able to order with confidence.

Our kitchen

All of our food is freshly prepared in a single kitchen that handles all 14 of the allergens named in UK law. Because ingredients and equipment are shared, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from any allergen, and there is always a risk of cross-contamination.

The 14 named allergens

  • Celery
  • Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats)
  • Crustaceans (e.g. prawns, crab)
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Lupin
  • Milk
  • Molluscs (e.g. mussels, squid)
  • Mustard
  • Tree nuts (e.g. almonds, walnuts)
  • Peanuts
  • Sesame
  • Soya
  • Sulphur dioxide / sulphites

Before you order

  • Tell us about your allergy or intolerance before placing your order — call us on {{shopPhone}}.
  • If you order online, please call to confirm what is safe before relying on your order; you can also add a note about your allergy.
  • Let us know how severe your allergy is so we can advise you properly.

If you ever have any doubt about whether a dish is safe for you, please ask us — we would always rather check than risk it.
